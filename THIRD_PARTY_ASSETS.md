# Third-party marks and icons

The MIT license covers original code, configuration examples, documentation and diagram layouts in this repository. Third-party logos, app icons and trademarks retain their respective owners’ rights and are not relicensed under MIT.

Icons identify tools in an editorial architecture diagram and do not imply endorsement. Official site or installed-app sources are recorded in `diagrams/logos/sources.json`. OpenAI and Claude family icons have explicit product labels. The Medium icon is a legacy symbol from its official logo repository. Review vendors’ current brand guidelines before reusing their marks.
